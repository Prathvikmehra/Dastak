# Privacy Policy - Dastak

> **DRAFT: needs review before store release**

## 1. Core Commitment: Local-Only by Default

Dastak is designed to protect your privacy completely:

- **Zero Remote Storage**: All your cases, notes, medicine records, and people profiles are stored exclusively on your device using local storage (`AsyncStorage`).
- **No Remote Servers**: Dastak runs completely offline. We do not operate any backend server to receive, process, or store your personal data.
- **Zero Analytics or Trackers**: We do not include any tracking SDKs, analytics packages, advertising IDs, or telemetry code. No event logging leaves your phone.

## 2. Information You Enter

When you use Dastak, you may input:

- Details of transactions, disputes, order numbers, or police complaint references.
- Medicines and costs you track.
- Names of family members for tracking unclaimed deposits or medicine lists.

This information never leaves your phone unless **you explicitly choose to share** a complaint draft via your device's native share sheet (e.g. sending via email, WhatsApp, or printing).

## 3. System Permissions

- **Notifications**: Used solely to schedule local alarms on your phone for case escalation ladder deadlines. We never send remote push notifications.
- **Phone / Web Links**: Used to open the phone dialer (e.g. for emergency cyber helpline 1930) or browser links to official government dispute portals.

## 4. Deleting Your Data

You have complete control over your data:

- Inside the app under **Settings and help > Privacy guarantee**, tap **Delete everything**.
- This completely wipes all cases, drafts, evidence items, medicine lists, family member records, and scheduled notification reminders.
- Uninstalling the app also removes all local application data stored on your device.

## 5. Contact & Questions

If you have questions about Dastak's privacy architecture or need review before public store release, please refer to the project repository or contact the project maintainers.
