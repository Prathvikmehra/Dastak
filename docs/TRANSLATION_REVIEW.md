# Translation Review Sign-Off

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All Hindi (`hi`) and Gujarati (`gu`) translations in Dastak are machine-drafted.
> Nothing ships to production until a native speaker signs off each screen.
> Use plain, everyday words (not formal or bureaucratic language), and keep helpline numbers and portal names in Latin script.
